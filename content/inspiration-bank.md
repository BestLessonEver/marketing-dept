## 2. Video/Reel Concepts (20)

### Student Moments (5)

1. **"First Lesson vs. Six Months Later"**
   Split screen of a student's very first attempt at a song vs. them playing it confidently months later. Use a trending transformation audio. No talking, just the contrast. Pure gold for parent engagement.

2. **"The Moment It Clicks"**
   Capture (with permission) the exact second a student nails something they've been working on. The smile, the fist pump, the look at the teacher. Slow-mo the reaction. Use a triumphant audio drop.

3. **"What They Played vs. What They Wanted to Play"**
   Student plays a simplified version of a popular song, then cut to the original track. Caption: "Give us 6 months." Ends with BLE logo and trial CTA.

4. **"Recital Walkout"**
   Film students walking out of the recital hall after performing, high-fiving teachers and parents. Athlete tunnel energy. Use hype walk-out music. Slow-mo optional but encouraged.

5. **"Guess the Song in 3 Notes"**
   A student plays the first 3 notes of a popular song on their instrument. Pause. "Can you guess it?" Let it play out. Interactive, shareable, shows off student skill.

### Teacher Features (4)

6. **"Day in the Life: Music Teacher Edition"**
   Follow a teacher from their morning coffee to teaching kids to gigging at night. Show the range - they're not just instructors, they're working musicians who happen to be incredible teachers.

7. **"Teacher Reacts to Student Playing Their Song"**
   A student secretly learns one of their teacher's original songs and plays it for them in a lesson. Film the teacher's reaction. Emotional. Shareable. Real.

8. **"Things Music Teachers Hear Every Day"**
   Teacher sits at their instrument, looks at camera, and lip-syncs/reenacts common student quotes: "Can I play that TikTok song?" "Is this gonna be on the test?" "My mom says I have to practice but I already did... yesterday."

9. **"How a Musician Hears Everyday Sounds"**
   Teacher walks through the studio, the parking lot, a grocery store - tapping rhythms on everything, finding melodies in beeps and buzzes. Shows musical brain in a fun, relatable way.

### Parent Reactions (3)

10. **"Film Your Parents' Face at the Recital"**
    Compilation of parent reactions during student performances. The tears, the pride, the "that's MY kid" whisper. Use a warm, emotional audio track. This is your highest-share-potential content.

11. **"What Parents Say vs. What They Mean"**
    "We'll try one lesson" = signed up for a year. "They're not really musical" = crying at the recital 3 months later. "We're just exploring options" = already told grandma.

12. **"Parent Testimonial But Make It Casual"**
    Don't do a stiff testimonial. Film a parent in the lobby casually talking about the difference they've seen. Keep it under 30 seconds. Real > polished.

### Studio Life (4)

13. **"Studio Tour But It's Set to Our Students Playing"**
    Walk through the studio, each room has a different student playing a different instrument. The audio shifts as you pass each door. Shows the energy and variety without saying a word.

14. **"The Lobby Cam"**
    Time-lapse of the lobby over a lesson day. Kids coming in nervous, leaving excited. Parents chatting. The vibe. Speed it up, put fun music over it.

15. **"POV: You Just Walked Into Best Lesson Ever"**
    First-person camera walking in the front door. Greeted warmly. Hear music from every room. See students laughing. Feel the energy. Caption: "This is what a music studio should feel like."

16. **"Close the Studio Challenge"**
    Film the last teacher leaving at night, turning off the lights in each room, locking up. Peaceful. Satisfying. Caption: "Another day of making musicians." Use a lo-fi closing time audio.

### Educational (4)

17. **"One Chord, Five Songs"**
    Teacher plays one chord progression and shows how 5 popular songs use the same pattern. Mind-blowing for non-musicians. Shareable. Makes music feel accessible.

18. **"What 1 Month of Practice Actually Sounds Like"**
    Set realistic expectations. Week 1: choppy. Week 2: getting there. Week 3: recognizable. Week 4: performing it. Manages parent expectations while showing real progress.

19. **"The Wrong Way to Practice vs. The Right Way"**
    30-second side by side. Left: kid zoning out, playing the same wrong note over and over. Right: kid playing slowly, fixing mistakes, building up. Short, valuable, shareable.

20. **"Myth vs. Reality: Music Lessons Edition"**
    Myth: "You need natural talent." Reality: shows a montage of regular kids absolutely shredding. Myth: "It takes years to sound good." Reality: shows first-month students playing recognizable songs.

## 5. Campaign Concepts (10)

### 1. "Stage Ready" - Spring Enrollment Push
- **Timing**: February - April
- **Tagline**: "From first note to first stage. Let's go."
- **Concept**: Frame spring enrollment as preparation for the summer recital. Every new student gets a "Stage Ready" roadmap showing what they'll be able to perform by summer. Social content shows last year's spring starters performing at the summer showcase.
- **Execution**: Landing page with "Stage Ready" countdown, email drip to leads, Facebook/Instagram ads featuring before-and-after student clips, in-studio posters with summer recital date.

### 2. "Summer Sessions" - Summer Camp & Lessons
- **Timing**: May - July
- **Tagline**: "The best part of their summer won't need sunscreen."
- **Concept**: Position summer lessons and camps as the antidote to summer brain rot. Highlight flexible scheduling, camp options, and the fact that kids come out of summer with a new skill instead of just a tan.
- **Execution**: Early bird pricing for May sign-ups, social countdown to summer, parent testimonial reel from last summer, camp-specific landing page.

### 3. "Encore Season" - Back to School
- **Timing**: August - September
- **Tagline**: "Add one thing to their schedule they won't complain about."
- **Concept**: Position lessons as the one extracurricular kids actually look forward to. Contrast with the dread of back-to-school. Feature students who came back energized after summer sessions.
- **Execution**: "Back to Music" social series, limited-time waived registration fee, school-partnership flyers, email blast to lapsed leads.

### 4. "Play It Forward" - Holiday Gift Campaign
- **Timing**: November - December
- **Tagline**: "Give a gift that doesn't need batteries."
- **Concept**: Gift lesson packages for the holidays. Target parents, grandparents, and adult self-gifters. Position music lessons as the meaningful alternative to more stuff.
- **Execution**: Gift certificate landing page, holiday-themed ad creative, "gift guide" email to full list, in-studio gift package display, Black Friday / Cyber Monday promo.

### 5. "Resolution Proof" - New Year Campaign
- **Timing**: January
- **Tagline**: "New year, new instrument, no quitting by February."
- **Concept**: Play on the resolution-failure trope. Our students stick around because lessons are actually fun. Highlight adult learners specifically - this is the year they stop saying "I've always wanted to learn."
- **Execution**: "No-Quit Guarantee" (first month free if they don't love it), social series debunking excuses, adult-focused ad set, email to cold leads.

### 6. "Recital Season" - Pre-Recital Hype
- **Timing**: 4-6 weeks before any recital
- **Concept**: Build hype like it's a concert tour. Announce the lineup. Post student "headshots." Create a setlist graphic. Make it feel like a real event because it is one.
- **Tagline**: "They've been rehearsing. You've been warned."
- **Execution**: Social countdown, student spotlight series, parent invite emails with RSVP, poster design for in-studio and social, post-recital highlight reel within 48 hours.

### 7. "Try Before You Buy" - Evergreen Trial Push
- **Timing**: Always running
- **Tagline**: "One lesson. Zero commitment. Full bragging rights."
- **Concept**: The free trial is always the primary CTA. This campaign keeps it front and center with rotating creative that features real students, real teachers, and real results. No stock photos, no fake smiles.
- **Execution**: Evergreen Facebook and Google ads, retargeting pixel on site visitors, trial booking page optimized monthly, rotating testimonials.

### 8. "Teacher Takeover" - Teacher Spotlight Month
- **Timing**: Any slow month (typically January or June)
- **Tagline**: "Meet the musicians behind the music."
- **Concept**: Each week, a different teacher "takes over" social media. They share their musical journey, what they love about teaching, clips of them performing, and a student shout-out. Humanizes the brand and shows off the caliber of instruction.
- **Execution**: Pre-filmed teacher interview clips, IG stories takeover, teacher-specific landing pages, email series introducing each teacher.

### 9. "Squad Goals" - Sibling/Friend Enrollment
- **Timing**: Anytime, pairs well with referral pushes
- **Tagline**: "Music is better with your people."
- **Concept**: Incentivize families to enroll siblings or friends together. Discount for second family member. Content shows siblings/friends learning together, playing duets, performing together at recitals.
- **Execution**: Sibling discount offer, social content featuring student duos, email to current families, in-studio flyers, referral program tie-in.

### 10. "Level Up" - Re-Engagement for Lapsed Students
- **Timing**: Quarterly
- **Tagline**: "Your instrument misses you."
- **Concept**: Win back students who dropped off. No guilt, no pressure. Just a reminder that their spot is open and their teacher remembers them. Include a "welcome back" incentive.
- **Execution**: Email sequence to lapsed students (3 emails over 2 weeks), personalized text from their former teacher, "come back" social posts, re-enrollment discount.

## 6. Referral Program Promo Ideas (10)

**Program: "Share the Stage" - $50 for you, $50 for them**

### In-Studio (3)

1. **The Lobby Board**: Physical "Share the Stage" board in the lobby with a leaderboard of top referrers. Update monthly. Top referrer gets a bonus prize (free month, merch, etc.). Kids are competitive - lean into it.

2. **Recital Referral Cards**: At every recital, hand out "Share the Stage" cards to attending friends and family. The card has the referring student's name pre-printed and a QR code to book a trial. Capture leads when the emotional high of watching a performance is at its peak.

3. **Practice Room Posters**: Simple, eye-catching posters in every practice room: "Know someone who'd love this? Share the Stage. You get $50. They get $50. Everybody wins." QR code to referral landing page.

### Digital (4)

4. **"Your Kid's Hype Person" Email**: Send current families an email that says: "Your kid is crushing it. Know another kid who could? Share the Stage and you both get $50 off next month." Include a personalized referral link and a one-click share button for text/social.

5. **Social Media Referral Drops**: Monthly post: "PSA: If you tell your friends about us and they sign up, you both get $50. That's not a typo. That's just how we roll." Keep it casual. Repost with different creative each month.

6. **Referral Milestone Emails**: Automated emails when a family hits referral milestones: 1 referral = thank you + $50. 3 referrals = "You're basically our marketing department" + bonus. 5 referrals = free month + shout-out.

7. **Google Review + Referral Combo**: After a student performs well or hits a milestone, send an email: "We'd love a Google review - and if you know someone who should be here, Share the Stage for $50 each." Two birds, one email.

### Event-Based (3)

8. **Recital "Bring a Friend" Night**: One recital per year is a "Bring a Friend" event. Every student is encouraged to invite one friend who doesn't take lessons. Those friends get a free trial card and the referring student gets entered into a raffle for a big prize (new instrument, free semester, etc.).

9. **Community Event Referral Booth**: At any community event or showcase, set up a "Share the Stage" booth. Current students can bring friends to try an instrument on the spot. Any trial booked at the event triggers the referral credit immediately.

10. **Seasonal Referral Blitz**: Pick one month per quarter and double the referral reward to $100/$100. Announce it like a flash sale: "This month only: Share the Stage is DOUBLED. $100 for you. $100 for them. Go tell everyone." Create urgency and buzz. Track results to see if the bump in incentive moves the needle.
