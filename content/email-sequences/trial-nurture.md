# Trial Lesson Nurture Sequence

**Audience**: Leads who expressed interest (form fill, ad click, inquiry) but have NOT booked a trial lesson.
**Goal**: Get them to book a free trial lesson.
**Tone**: Irreverent, warm, zero-pressure. Cool friend energy.
**Sequence Length**: 5 emails over 14 days

---

## Email 1 - Day 0: Welcome

**Subject Line**: So you're thinking about music lessons
**Alt Subject**: Hey, glad you found us

**Preview Text**: We promise not to be weird about it.

**Body**:

Hey there,

You clicked a thing, so now we exist in your inbox. Don't worry -- we're not going to spam you with motivational quotes about music and the soul. (You're welcome.)

Here's the short version: Best Lesson Ever is a music studio in Friendswood where kids (and adults) actually look forward to their lessons. Our teachers are working musicians who gig on weekends and teach because they genuinely love it -- not because they're killing time between "real jobs."

We do guitar, piano, drums, voice, violin, bass, and ukulele. Lessons are one-on-one, and we give students way more chances to perform on real stages than anywhere else around here.

The easiest way to see if we're a fit? Book a free trial lesson. No commitment, no awkward sales pitch, no blood oath.

**CTA**: Book a Free Trial

**Send Time**: Within 1 hour of lead capture (immediate trigger)

**Why This Works**: Sets the tone immediately -- we're not a corporate music factory. The self-aware humor about email marketing disarms skepticism. Low-pressure CTA with explicit "no commitment" language removes friction. Establishes the three differentiators (working musicians, fun lessons, performance opportunities) without belaboring them.

## Email 3 - Day 5: Value-Add

**Subject Line**: Not sure which instrument? Here's a cheat sheet
**Alt Subject**: A quick guide before your kid's first lesson

**Preview Text**: Spoiler: there's no wrong answer.

**Body**:

One of the most common questions we get: "My kid wants to play music but we don't know which instrument to start with."

Totally normal. Here's our honest take:

**Piano** -- Great starting point for any age. Builds music theory naturally. Hard to sound terrible on day one.

**Guitar** -- The "cool factor" instrument. Kids 7+ do well. Sore fingers for week one, then smooth sailing.

**Drums** -- High energy kids thrive here. Yes, it's loud. Yes, it's worth it.

**Voice** -- No gear to buy. Perfect for kids who sing in the car constantly (you know the ones).

**Violin** -- Beautiful instrument, real talk: takes patience early on. Best for kids who like a challenge.

**Ukulele** -- Easiest win. Your kid will play a real song in lesson one. Confidence builder.

**Bass** -- The underrated pick. Instant groove, and every band needs one.

Honestly? The best instrument is whichever one your kid can't stop talking about. We can help figure that out in a trial lesson.

**CTA**: Book a Trial - We'll Help Them Choose

**Send Time**: Saturday, 9:00 AM CT

**Why This Works**: Genuinely useful content that answers a real question parents have. No selling -- just helpful guidance that positions BLE as knowledgeable and approachable. The CTA ties naturally to the content: "not sure? come find out." Saturday morning hits parents during weekend planning mode.

## Sequence Setup & Configuration

### Trigger Conditions
- Lead enters sequence when:
  - They submit a contact/inquiry form on bestlessonever.com
  - They click a Facebook or Google ad and submit lead info
  - They are manually added as a lead in Studio OS
- Lead must NOT have an existing trial lesson booked
- Lead must NOT be a current or former student

### Exit Conditions (Remove from Sequence Immediately)
- Lead books a trial lesson (primary exit)
- Lead enrolls as a member (skip trial)
- Lead unsubscribes
- Lead marks email as spam
- Lead replies asking to be removed

### Recommended Email Platform Setup
- **Platform**: Mailchimp, ConvertKit, or ActiveCampaign all work. ActiveCampaign is ideal for automation triggers tied to Studio OS data.
- **List/Segment**: Create a segment called "Trial Nurture - Active" for leads in this sequence
- **From Name**: Best Lesson Ever (or "BLE" if character-limited)
- **From Email**: hello@bestlessonever.com
- **Reply-To**: Same (monitor replies -- some leads will respond directly)
- **Footer**: Include studio address (Friendswood, TX), unsubscribe link, and a one-liner: "You're getting this because you showed interest in music lessons. No hard feelings if you unsubscribe."

### A/B Testing Suggestions

**Round 1 - Subject Lines**
- Test primary vs. alt subject lines for each email
- Measure open rates; run for at least 200 sends per variant before calling a winner

**Round 2 - CTA Copy**
- Test "Book a Free Trial" vs. "See Available Times" vs. "Try a Lesson Free"
- Measure click-through rate on the CTA button

**Round 3 - Send Times**
- Test morning (9-10 AM) vs. early afternoon (12-1 PM) for Emails 2-4
- Measure open rate and click rate

**Round 4 - Email Length**
- Test current copy vs. a shorter version (100-150 words) for Emails 2 and 3
- Measure click-through and trial booking rate

### Key Metrics to Track
- **Open rate** by email (benchmark: 35-45% for local service businesses)
- **Click-through rate** by email (benchmark: 3-5%)
- **Sequence-to-trial conversion rate**: % of leads entering the sequence who book a trial
- **Revenue attribution**: Track which email drove the trial booking (last-click)
- **Unsubscribe rate** by email (flag if any single email exceeds 1%)
